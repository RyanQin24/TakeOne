# A little disagreement

Define what each character wants and the change in their exchange. Maintain eyelines and screen direction. Give dialogue room to breathe; cut on a response when useful. Stage each character and explicit offscreen eyeline targets. Fictional tool preferences are character opinions, not performance evidence. Camera movement should support the exchange without prescribing a dolly or orbit.
